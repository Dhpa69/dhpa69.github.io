All in 1 IPTV – Support

All in 1 IPTV is a media player for your own M3U playlist, Xtream Codes account, or Stalker/Ministra portal. It does not include any channels or content.

Getting started

  1. Open the App and continue past the welcome notice.
  2. In Settings → Add provider, choose M3U / M3U8, Xtream, or Portal.
  3. Enter the details from your provider, then open Live TV to watch.

Premium

Premium is $1.99/month with a 1-week free trial, billed through Apple. Cancel anytime in Apple ID → Subscriptions.
