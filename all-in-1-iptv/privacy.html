All in 1 IPTV – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

All in 1 IPTV (“the App”) is a media player for Apple TV, iPhone, iPad and Apple Vision Pro, published by Dhaval Patel. It plays streams from a playlist or account you already have. The App does not provide, sell or include any channels or content.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal data from the App. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter of ours, and no account system run by us. Playlists, provider logins, favorites and settings stay on your device. Favorites and settings may sync through your own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit / key-value storage), which is controlled by Apple under your Apple ID — that is not collection by us.

Subscriptions

Premium access is sold as an auto-renewable subscription through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Manage or cancel in Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Content you add

URLs, usernames and passwords you enter for your own playlist or provider are stored on your device (and in your iCloud if sync is on) so the App can play your streams. We never see them.

Children

The App is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
